Our popular Bond (Scandinavian look) line is simple in form yet at the same time combines tradition with modernity.

The Select steel roofing system is a twist on the traditional trapeze panel. Home owners in Toronto have to worry about replacing their shingle roof every 6-10 years. Storm damage, snow and high winds can blow shingles off the roof, exposing your home to the elements and pests. Rather than making spot repairs and spending money constantly on your shingled roof, replace it with a Select metal roof. Our professionally trained installation crews will be on time, safe and have full liability and WSIB coverage.

Rudan Roofing systems are produced east of Toronto with Swedish or German metal. Both coil metal manufactures are leaders in high durability metal production. An advantage of a Stellar Metal roof is the metal coils have a thick layer of zinc coating (275g/m2) on both sides of your roofing or cladding. This extra thick zinc coating protects the steel core from corrosion, especially when scratched or cut. The core thickness of the steel is min 0.55 mm- 25 gauge, the thickest in the industry. Metal roofing has become extremely popular due to its naturally attractive appearance and its ability to self repair and avoid corrosion. Our European metal is available in a variety of colours and finishes. Visit our facility to see all of the options available for your metal project.
